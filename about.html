About Odisha Cancer Care

A charitable organization supporting people and families affected by cancer.

Who we are

Odisha Cancer Care Trust is a dedicated, non-profit organization committed to transforming oncology care across the region. Rooted in compassion and guided by medical excellence, we bridge the gap between advanced cancer treatments and the communities that need them most. From early detection awareness drives in rural districts to providing financial, nutritional, and emotional aid to underprivileged families, our trust stands as a reliable pillar of strength for patients navigating their cancer journey.

Our mission

Our mission is to make comprehensive cancer care accessible, affordable, and equitable for every individual in Odisha. We strive to reduce cancer mortality through relentless community awareness, early screening initiatives, state-of-the-art diagnostic support, and holistic patient assistance programs that leave no patient behind.

Our vision

We envision a future where no life in Odisha is lost due to late cancer diagnosis or lack of affordable treatment. We aim to build a cancer-resilient society powered by compassionate healthcare, strong community support, and empowering hope at every stage of healing.

Our values

Respect and privacy for every patient and family.

Care and understanding in difficult times.
