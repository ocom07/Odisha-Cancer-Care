Why Support Odisha Cancer Care?

How contributions are intended to help — and how we aim to stay accountable.

Our mission

At Odisha Cancer Care, our mission is to ensure that no cancer patient or family in Odisha faces the journey alone. We are committed to providing compassionate financial aid, guidance, and emotional support to ensure timely treatment and care for underprivileged families.

How we assist

We follow a transparent, verified approach to ensure support reaches those who need it most. Our medical and social review panel carefully verifies patient credentials and medical documentation, allowing us to directly coordinate financial assistance, facilitate access to government schemes, and offer guidance every step of the way.

Frequently asked questions

How do I donate?

Ask for an acknowledgement or receipt when you contact the organization. We do not promise any tax benefit unless eligibility is confirmed and documented.
