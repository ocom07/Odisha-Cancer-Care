How to Get Free Cancer Treatment in Odisha: GJAY, Ayushman Card and Helplines

A practical guide to checking government health-cover eligibility, understanding treatment at public hospitals, using the Swasthya Mitra help desk and finding the right helpline before a cancer-treatment visit.

7 November 2026 · Odisha Cancer Care Editorial Team · Medical review: pending

A cancer diagnosis can bring urgent medical decisions and financial worries at the same time. In Odisha, government health programmes may help eligible patients access treatment at public hospitals and, where applicable, empanelled private hospitals. The exact benefit depends on the scheme, hospital, package and the patient's eligibility, so always confirm before admission.

1. Start with the hospital and scheme desk

At a government hospital, ask the registration counter or patient-help desk where cancer patients can check scheme support. At an empanelled private hospital, ask for the Swasthya Mitra or scheme help desk. Request a written explanation of eligibility, covered procedures, limits, exclusions and any amount that may remain payable.

2. Understand GJAY support

The Government of Odisha's GJAY information says government bears the cost of specified health services in public health institutions, including cancer chemotherapy, subject to the programme's rules. It also describes cashless treatment at empanelled private hospitals for eligible card-holding families, within applicable annual coverage limits. Do not assume every hospital, medicine, test or treatment plan is automatically covered.

3. What documents should you carry?

Carry the patient's Aadhaar or another accepted identity document, ration card or eligible health card, mobile number linked to the card if applicable, recent prescriptions, biopsy and scan reports, discharge summaries, referral letters, previous bills and passport-size photographs if requested. Keep photocopies and digital copies. Ask the help desk whether any additional documents are required.

4. Helplines and official information

For general GJAY enquiries, the official dashboard lists 104. For smart-card-related queries it lists 155369. Call to confirm current instructions and ask the hospital to verify the live eligibility and package status. You can also check the official GJAY dashboard and hospital directory.

5. Before you leave the hospital

Ask for the name of the scheme desk contact, the package or approval status, what is covered, what is not covered, and whom to contact if a cashless request is delayed. Never pay an unofficial agent to obtain scheme benefits.

Important: Scheme rules and empanelled-hospital lists can change. Confirm eligibility and treatment coverage directly with the hospital's authorised help desk before making financial decisions.
