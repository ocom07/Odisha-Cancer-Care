A Caregiver’s Guide: Supporting a Family Member with Cancer and Finding Financial Help

Practical ideas for organising appointments, medicines, travel, emotional support and scheme enquiries—plus ways to contact Odisha Cancer Care for guidance.

7 November 2026 · Odisha Cancer Care Editorial Team · Medical review: pending

When someone in a family is diagnosed with cancer, caregivers often become the organisers, listeners and problem-solvers. You do not need to know everything. A few simple systems can make appointments, treatment and day-to-day support easier to manage.

Organise the medical information

Keep a folder with prescriptions, pathology and scan reports, discharge summaries, medicine lists, appointment dates and doctors' contact instructions. Write down questions before each visit and ask one family member to keep the records updated.

Support without taking away independence

Ask the patient what kind of help they want. Offer to accompany them, prepare meals, arrange transport or help communicate with relatives. Listen without forcing optimism. Respect privacy and include the patient in decisions whenever possible.

Plan travel and stays

Before travelling, confirm the appointment, reporting time, hospital location and documents required. Ask the hospital social-work desk or patient-help desk whether any patient accommodation, transport guidance or support service is available. Keep a small bag ready with medicines, water, reports and emergency contacts.

Explore financial assistance

Ask the hospital's authorised scheme desk about GJAY or other applicable government support, eligibility, empanelment and package approval. Odisha's State Treatment Fund and other assistance programmes may have separate eligibility and application rules. Get the current forms and instructions from the official department or hospital; do not rely on unofficial agents.

How Odisha Cancer Care may be able to help

Families can contact Odisha Cancer Care to ask about the support and guidance the organisation currently provides. Assistance depends on available resources, eligibility and verification; contact does not guarantee funding or treatment coverage. You can also review the organisation's Urgent Cases page for cases currently listed.

Important: Caregivers need support too. Ask for help early, keep records organised and confirm every financial-assistance condition with the official scheme or hospital.
