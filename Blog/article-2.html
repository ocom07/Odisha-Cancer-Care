Cancer Hospitals in Odisha: Where to Go for Treatment

An impartial starting point for exploring cancer-care services in Cuttack, Bhubaneswar and other districts, with a checklist of questions and documents to take to your first appointment.

7 November 2026 · Odisha Cancer Care Editorial Team · Medical review: pending

The right place to begin depends on the cancer type, stage, urgency, required treatment and the services available at the hospital. Rather than relying on online rankings, speak with a qualified oncology team and compare the facilities that can provide the care your doctor recommends.

1. Government cancer-care options

Acharya Harihar Post Graduate Institute of Cancer (AHPGIC), formerly widely referred to as AHRCC, is a specialist government cancer institute in Cuttack. Other public institutions, including SCB Medical College and Hospital in Cuttack and AIIMS Bhubaneswar, may provide relevant specialist services. Service availability, referral requirements and waiting times can vary; contact the hospital before travelling.

2. Care closer to your district

A district hospital, government medical college or nearby health facility can help with an initial assessment, referrals, selected investigations and follow-up. Ask whether the service needed—such as surgery, chemotherapy, radiotherapy, pathology or palliative care—is available there or requires referral to another centre.

3. Private hospitals and empanelment

Private cancer centres may offer different specialists and treatment facilities. If you plan to use GJAY or another health-cover scheme, verify that the specific hospital and the required treatment package are currently empanelled. Ask for a written estimate and a clear explanation of what is covered before starting treatment.

4. Questions to ask before choosing

Who will coordinate the treatment plan? Is a multidisciplinary team available? Which tests are needed before treatment? How soon can treatment begin? Are chemotherapy, radiotherapy and surgery available on site? What are the likely costs and scheme options? Who can be contacted after hours if a complication occurs?

5. First-visit document checklist

Carry identity and health-card documents, referral letter, biopsy or pathology reports, imaging reports and films/CDs, blood-test results, prescriptions, previous discharge summaries, a list of current medicines and allergies, and contact details of a family caregiver. Keep originals safe and submit copies when possible.

Important: A hospital name alone does not tell you whether it can provide the exact treatment needed. Confirm the relevant specialist, service, appointment process and financial arrangements directly.
