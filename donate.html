Donate Now

Make a Difference Today

Your willingness to help can make a meaningful difference to people and families facing cancer. Contact Odisha Cancer Care through its official WhatsApp number to request the verified donation details.

How it works

  1. Click the “I WANT TO DONATE” button.
  2. WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message to Odisha Cancer Care.
  3. Tap Send to contact the organization.
  4. The authorized admin replies manually with the official payment instructions.
  5. Follow the verified instructions and request an acknowledgement or receipt, where applicable.

Please read before donating

  • Opening WhatsApp does not complete a donation.
  • This website processes no payments and does not verify transactions or issue receipts automatically.
  • Verify any payment instructions you receive with the organization’s official contact details.
  • Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, password or other banking credentials with anyone.
  • No tax benefit is promised unless eligibility is confirmed and documented. See the Donation Disclaimer.
